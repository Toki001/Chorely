# Initial Chore Template Library (Illustrative, guardian-configured)

**Safety note:** Parents/guardians choose age-appropriate activities and provide supervision as needed. These are examples only, not universal age recommendations.

| Category | Suggested chore | Optional checklist | Time preference |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dinner | Cook rice | Prepare ingredients/equipment safely, follow household instructions, turn off appliance | Before dinner |
| Dinner | Set table | Plates, utensils, water | Before mealtime |
| Dinner | Wash dishes | Clear plates, wash/sanitize as household practices, wipe surface | After dinner |
| Pets | Feed family pet | Confirm instructions and last feeding; record completion | Configured pet schedule |
| Pets | Refill pet water | Check bowl, refill clean water | Daily/configurable |
| Kitchen | Wipe counters | Use household-approved supplies | After food prep |
| Bedrooms | Tidy your room | Put away items, gather laundry | Daily/weekly |
| Living space | Sweep floor | Sweep designated safe zone | Weekly |
| Waste | Take trash to designated area | Follow household safety guidance | Collection day |
| Supplies | Check soap/detergent | Mark low/out when appropriate | Weekly |
| Household | Water plants | Check care notes first | Configurable |
| Household | Fold clean laundry | Sort family items | Weekly |

Do not preassign hazardous cleaning chemicals, unsafe cooking or heavy lifting to child profiles. Avoid diagnoses, medicine dosing, unsupervised dangerous appliances. Parents own suitability decisions. Chore estimates and effort weighting are adjustable, never authoritative.
